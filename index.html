Manual Local Storage Mode

100% Private Offline

Your financial data is saved directly in your browser's persistent storage (localStorage). No login, no Google authentication, and no automatic background cloud uploads are running.

📁 How to Manage Your Data Manually Across Devices

  • Creating Backups: Click Download JSON Backup to save your accounts, transactions, custom categories, and savings goals into a timestamped file.
  • Moving to Another Device: Send the downloaded .json file to your phone, tablet, or secondary PC, and click Restore from File.
  • Spreadsheet Audits: Use the Spreadsheet & Backup tab or click Download Spreadsheet (.csv) to analyze your records in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Zero Tracking: Since no external accounts are connected, your ledger is strictly accessible only on the browser and device you use.
